*Exemplary Performance is subject to GGSIPU eligibility conditions, including passing all courses in the first attempt. This calculator cannot verify them, so a 10.00 is shown as conditional. Division is officially based on the overall CGPA at the completion of the programme.

Formula

SGPA = Σ(Credit × Grade Point) ÷ Σ(Credits)
  1. Each subject’s grade points are multiplied by its credits.
  2. The weighted points are added together.
  3. The total is divided by the included credits.

Assumptions

Every subject you enter is treated as a credit-bearing course and included in the denominator. Audit, non-credit and zero-credit courses are not modelled; leave them out. Failed (F) subjects score 0 points but still count their credits. The SGPA is kept unrounded internally, and only the display is rounded, so an unrounded 3.99 is never promoted to Third Division.
